## Funding
Your time and skills are valuable; you should never work for free.

* [How to apply for and get compute grants (for students)](https://nightingal3.github.io/blog/2026/04/16/compute-grants/)


Here are some options for UMBC students:
* <a href="https://ur.umbc.edu/prestigious-scholarships/goldwater-scholars/" aria-label="Goldwater Scholars program">Goldwater Scholars </a> (undergrad only)
* <a href="https://ur.umbc.edu/urcad/" aria-label="URCAD">Undergraduate Research and Creative Achievement Day (URCAD) </a> (undergrad only)
* <a href="https://ur.umbc.edu/ura/" aria-label="undergraduate research awards">Undergraduate Research Awards (URA)</a> (undergrad only)
* <a href="https://ur.umbc.edu/summer-research/" aria-label="summer research scholarships">Summer Research Scholarships</a> (undergrad only)
* <a href="https://umbc.edu/admissions/graduate/funding" aria-label="graduate fellowships">Graduate Fellowships (grad only)</a>
* Research for course credit. This does not pay you, but getting credit for doing research can be a form of compensation.
  * <a href="https://www.csee.umbc.edu/independent-study/" aria-label="independent study"> CMSC 499/699: Independent Study</a>
  * CMSC 698: Project in Computer Science
  * CMSC 799: Masters Thesis
* Working in collaboration with another faculty member who has funding.

External funding opportunities:
* <a href="https://allenai.org/predoctoral-young-investigators" aria-label="AI2 predoctoral young investigators">Allen Institute for AI's Predoctoral Young Investigators program</a> (undergrad and masters only)
* <a href="https://cra.org/cra-wp/dreu/" aria-label="CRA-WP Distributed Research Experiences for Undergraduates">CRA-WP Distributed Research Experiences for Undergraduates (DREU)</a> (undergrad only)
* <a href="https://cra.org/cra-wp/dream-distributed-research-apprenticeships-for-masters-top/" aria-label="NSF DREAM">NSF DREAM</a> (masters only)
* <a href="https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/dcl-computer-information-science-engineering-graduate" aria-label="NSF CS grad 4 US">NSF's CSGrad4US Fellowship</a> (graduate only; must be US citizen/resident)
* <a href="https://www.nsfgrfp.org/" aria-label="NSF GRFP">NSF's Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)</a> (graduate only; must be US citizen/resident)
* <a href="https://www.hertzfoundation.org/" aria-label="Hertz fellowship">Hertz Fellowship</a> (first or second year PhD students)
* <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/company/values/tech-at-bloomberg/data-science/academic-engagement-programs/data-science-ph-d-fellowship/" aria-label="Bloomberg's Data Science PhD Fellowship">Bloomberg's Data Science PhD Fellowship</a> (PhD student)
* <a href="https://research.google/programs-and-events/phd-fellowship/" aria-label="Google PhD Fellowship">Google PhD Fellowship</a> (PhD student)
* <a href="https://research.ibm.com/labs/yorktown-heights/ibm-academic-awards-program" aria-label="IBM PhD Fellowship">IBM PhD Fellowship</a> (PhD student)

* <a href="https://linksim.org/program-guidelines/" aria-label="Link Foundation">Link Foundation</a> (PhD student)
